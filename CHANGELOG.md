## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-08

First release: a transaction-level SystemC model of a tiled INT8/INT4 NPU cluster,
its SoC platforms, the `npuperf` analysis tools and generated studies on four design points.

### Added

- Configuration in nested groups (`shape`, `arch`, `mapping`, `weights`, `quant`, `sim`) from
  one option table that drives the command line, `--help`, `--config FILE.json` and the
  complete `config` echo of every result (schema version 2); `--describe-config`, `--version`.
- Requantization modes `tosa-single` and `tosa-double`, bit-exact to TOSA 1.0.2
  `apply_scale_32` and to the TFLite, gemmlowp and CMSIS-NN formulations; signed per-channel
  shifts in `quant.shifts` and `NPUQ1` files.
- Grouped GEMM (`--groups`), with depthwise convolution as $G$ groups of $N = 1$.
- Depthwise datapath (`--depthwise-macs-per-cycle`): channel-spanning tiles at their own MAC
  rate.
- Implicit convolution (`--conv-*`): the activation operand is the im2col view of an NHWC
  input, and panel loads read each tile's input footprint.
- Activation-panel cache (`--activation-cache-bytes`) beside the weight-panel cache, both in
  the SRAM budget.
- Output- and weight-stationary systolic array timing (`--dataflow`).
- Reduction-panel double buffering (`--panel-buffers`); the weight decoder runs ahead of the
  MAC datapath.
- AXI4 memory model (`--memory-model axi`): independent read and write channels, INCR bursts
  split at the burst limit and 4 KiB, outstanding limits, read and write-response latencies,
  partial beats, and one DRAM reservation timeline in exact byte units with a fixed grant
  order; per-channel statistics.
- External-memory address map; the memory system is a module behind `MemoryPort`.
- Multi-core cluster (`--cores`, `--partition m|n|auto`) on one shared DRAM timeline, with a
  closed-form traffic and makespan estimate per split.
- `--timing-only` runs up to $10^{13}$ MACs; dimensions up to 1,048,576; bit-exact runs up to
  $4 \times 10^9$ MACs.
- Transformer engine: batched prefill, a DRAM-resident KV cache with DMA-charged reads and
  writes, TTFT and TPOT, SRAM-sized weight panels.
- `npuperf` package: `analytic.analyze()` and the request-level replay `analytic.schedule()`,
  `mapper`, `designs`, `studies` and `reproduce`, roofline and SVG figures, sweep, autotune,
  energy, graph runtime, graph optimizer, ONNX importer.
- Four design points (`configs/`) and layer tables of MobileNetV2, ResNet-50 v1.5, BERT-base
  and Llama-3.2-1B prefill and decode (`workloads/`).
- Studies S1 to S10 in `docs/results.md`, regenerated by `python3 -m npuperf.reproduce`.
- Documentation: architecture, numerics, SoC integration, design points, results,
  methodology and usage.
- CMake presets; CI on GCC, Clang and MSVC across Linux x64 and Arm64, macOS and Windows, with
  sanitizer, coverage, ONNX Runtime, results-drift and lint jobs.
- Apache-2.0 license.
